# Codex Universalis — Live Collaborative Classroom ## CSI KJSSE Renaissance Buildathon | 16 Oct 2026 --- ## Slide 1 — Title **CODEX UNIVERSALIS** Speak, and knowledge becomes art — in every language. A real-time collaborative classroom where teachers teach, students learn, and language is never a barrier. Built in one day by two people | CSI KJSSE Renaissance Buildathon --- ## Slide 2 — The Problem - Teachers speak in mixed English/Hindi/Marathi (Hinglish) - Students miss concepts due to language gaps - Diagrams take too long to draw by hand - No live connection between teacher's board and student's notebook --- ## Slide 3 — The Solution **One platform, four pillars:** 1. **Human Potential** — Divine Dictation: Speak a concept → instant Mermaid.js diagram 2. **Knowledge Without Borders** — Rosetta Engine: Hinglish/Hindi/Marathi → pure academic English automatically 3. **A New Worldview** — Live collaborative classroom: Teacher types/draws, every student sees it instantly 4. **Challenge the Perspective** — Avatar Professor: Upload a face, get Revision Mode summaries spoken by your avatar --- ## Slide 4 — How It Works ``` Teacher (Maestro) Students (Apprentices) ┌─────────────┐ ┌─────────────┐ │ Type notes │ ──WebSocket──> │ Live feed │ │ Speak → │ │ Diagrams │ │ Diagram │ │ Whiteboard │ │ Draw → │ │ Revision │ │ Whiteboard │ │ Summary │ └─────────────┘ └─────────────┘ ``` --- ## Slide 5 — Key Features - **Live Notes:** Teacher's screen → all student screens instantly - **Voice-to-Diagram:** Say "the water cycle" → flowchart renders automatically - **Auto-Translation:** Hinglish classroom speech → clean English on student screens - **Shared Whiteboard:** Teacher draws, every student sees strokes in real-time - **Revision Mode:** One-click 3-sentence lecture recap with talking avatar - **Private Marginalia:** Students take private notes that never leave their browser --- ## Slide 6 — Tech Stack - **FastAPI** + **WebSockets** — real-time backend - **NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 120B** — translation + diagram generation + revision summaries - **Mermaid.js** — hand-drawn flowchart rendering - **Web Speech API** — voice dictation - **Vanilla JS** — no build steps, deploys anywhere --- ## Slide 7 — Demo - Maestro speaks → diagram appears on every student screen - Teacher draws on whiteboard → students see it live - One click → revision summary with talking avatar - All under 3 minutes, zero setup --- ## Slide 8 — Impact - Removes language barriers in mixed-language classrooms - Makes abstract concepts visual instantly - Gives every student a personal revision tutor - Works on any device with a browser --- ## Slide 9 — Team & Timeline - **Team:** 2 students, SY Robotics & AI, KJ Somaiya College of Engineering - **Built:** 1 day, single hackathon session - **Status:** Working prototype, ready to scale **Speak, and knowledge becomes art — in every language.** --- ## Slide 10 — Questions? **Codex Universalis** — Renaissance Buildathon GitHub: github.com/shuklaabhinav091-glitch/codex-universalis
